Formula key

Net sales — Σ net_sales over REPORTED entities (stores + e-commerce). A store that has not posted is excluded, never zero-filled.

Comp % — (Σ comp-store TY ÷ Σ comp-store LY-aligned) − 1. LY-aligned = same fiscal week, same weekday; holiday codes override the week alignment.

Plan attainment — Σ reported actual ÷ Σ plan for the same entities.

Contribution to comp gap — (entity TY − entity LY-aligned) ÷ total comp LY-aligned. The focus panel's unit; named contributions plus 'all other' equal the headline gap exactly.

Completeness — reported stores ÷ expected open stores; posted sales ÷ trailing-4 same-weekday average.

AOV / UPT / AUR — net ÷ transactions, units ÷ transactions, net ÷ units.
